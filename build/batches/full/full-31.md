## D.D.M. - Different Dimension Master
[effect Level 5 LIGHT Spellcaster 1700/1500] formats: edison
Text (Edison): Once per turn, you can discard 1 Spell Card to Special Summon 1 of your removed from play monsters.
Rulings: 1. Discarding a Spell Card is a cost. The effect of this monster targets a face-up monster that is removed from play. If your opponent controls your monster because of "Creature Swap" and it is removed from play, you can Special Summon it with "D.D.M. - Different Dimension Master" because it is in your removed zone. ## Damage Condenser
[trap Normal] formats: edison
Text (Edison): When you take battle damage: Discard 1 card; Special Summon 1 monster from your Deck with ATK less than or equal to the battle damage you took, in Attack Position.
Rulings: 1. If there are no cards left in your Deck, you cannot activate "Damage Condenser".
2. You cannot use "Damage Condenser" to Special Summon a monster whose ATK is variable or undetermined ("King of the Skull Servants", "Maju Garzett", etc.)
3. Discarding a card is a cost to activate "Damage Condenser".
4. "Damage Condenser" is activated during the Damage Step immediately after you take battle damage. Because "Kuriboh" is activated before this (during damage calculation), you cannot discard "Kuriboh" to prevent damage and still activate "Damage Condenser" when taking damage for the same attack.
5. If your opponent attacks you directly with "The Bistro Butcher" while you have no cards in your hand, you cannot draw 2 cards for "The Bistro Butcher" then discard to activate "Damage Condenser"; you've missed the chance to activate it.

## Damage Translation
[trap Normal] formats: edison
Text (Edison): Halve all effect damage you take this turn. During the End Phase this turn, Special Summon 1 "Ghost Token" (Fiend/DARK/Level 1/ATK 0/DEF 0) in Defense Position for each time you took effect damage this turn.
Rulings: 1. If you would take damage during the End Phase after you've Special Summoned the 'Ghost Tokens' the damage is still halved but you will not be able to Summon any more 'Ghost Tokens.'
2. If the effect of "Damage Translation" is being applied and you switch damage you would have taken to your opponent with "Barrel Behind the Door" the damage is halved. (Example: I activate "Damage Translation". Later in the turn, you activate "Ookazi" and I Chain with "Barrel Behind the Door". You will only take 400 damage.)
3. If you take damage more times than the number of open Monster Card Zones you have, you cannot Special Summon any 'Ghost Tokens'.
4. The effect that Special Summons the 'Ghost Tokens' starts a Chain.
5. Even if you activate 2 copies of "Damage Translation" in the same turn, the damage will only be halved.
