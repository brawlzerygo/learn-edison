[spell Continuous] formats: edison
Text (Edison): Monster Cards Tributed for a Ritual Summon are returned to the owner's Deck instead of being sent to the GY.
Edison rulings: ●The effect of “Samsara” does not start a chain. The Tributed monsters will be shuffled into the Deck immediately, instead of being sent to the GY. Then the Ritual Monster is Special Summoned.
●If “Samsara” and “Banisher of the Light” are both on the field the monsters Tributed for a Ritual Summon will be shuffled back into the Deck.
●If “Dark Magician of Chaos” is used as a Tribute for a Ritual Summon while “Samsara” is on the field it will be shuffled back into the Deck.

## Samsara Lotus
[effect Level 1 DARK Plant 0/0] formats: edison
Text (Edison): During your End Phase, if you control no Spell or Trap Cards, you can Special Summon this card from your GY in Attack Position. This card's controller takes 1000 damage during each of their Standby Phases.
Edison rulings: ●Both of Samsara Lotus' effects are Trigger Effects.
●If you chain a Quick-Play Spell Card from your hand to your Samsara Lotus' Special Summon effect, Samsara Lotus' effect disappears.
●If Book of Moon, etc., is chained to Samsara Lotus' damage inflicting effect and flips it face-down, the effect disappears.

## Seed Cannon
[spell Continuous] formats: edison
Text (Edison): Each time a Plant monster(s) is Summoned, place 1 Plant Counter on this card (max. 5). You can send this card to the GY to inflict 500 damage to your opponent for each Plant Counter on it.
Rulings: 1. Placing a Plant Counter on this card does not start a chain.
2. Sending "Seed Cannon" to the GY is a cost.
3. If there are no Plant Counters on "Seed Cannon", you cannot activate its effect and send it to the GY.
